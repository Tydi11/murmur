Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026.

Who runs Murmur

Murmur is a private app run by an individual for their own household on a home server. It is not offered to the public.

What Murmur stores

The journal entries, voice recordings, tasks, events and lists that each person creates. All of it is stored on the household's own server and is visible only to the person who created it, except shopping lists that the household chooses to share between its members.

Google user data

If a person chooses to connect their Google account, Murmur asks for permission to see the list of their calendars and to create, change and delete events on calendars they choose. Murmur uses this only to:

  • add the events and reminders that person saved in Murmur to the calendar they picked, and update or remove them when they change them in Murmur;
  • show that person their own upcoming events inside Murmur.

Google user data is stored only on the household's server, is never sold, never shared with anyone else, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. Murmur's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Services Murmur uses

To turn a voice note into text and organise it, the recording is sent to a speech-to-text service and the resulting text to an AI model provider, which process it and return the result. No calendar data is sent to these services.

Removing your data

A person can disconnect Google from Murmur's settings at any time, which deletes the stored Google access from the server. You can also remove Murmur's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Entries can be deleted from inside the app.
